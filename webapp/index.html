This is the simulated CP read from a saved flight — the median of that simulation's CP location column, dropping launch-rod and post-landing rows and anything below Mach 0.05. It is not the static CP that OpenRocket's design view draws: that one is the worst case over 360 roll angles at Mach 0.3 and zero angle of attack, and it can sit tens of millimetres forward of this number.
